Business Model and Growth Drivers

Alexandria RE specializes in life science real estate, leasing high-quality laboratories and office space to biotech, pharmaceutical, and tech firms. This focus provides somewhat of a recession-resistant moat, given the persistent demand for innovation space.

Growth is fueled by strategic land acquisitions in key innovation clusters like Boston, San Francisco, and New York, paired with long-term tenant leases. The company also benefits from the accelerating trend of biotech startups seeking cutting-edge lab spaces, which shouldn't slow even if overall markets wobble.
