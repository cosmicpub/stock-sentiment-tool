Digital Transformation

The company has accelerated investment in online sales channels, which are becoming crucial to capture younger buyers. While not leading the pack in e-commerce innovation, AutoNation’s strategy to blend online convenience with dealerships’ local service advantage positions it well for evolving consumer habits.
