Hasbro’s challenge is to innovate its product offerings while preserving its iconic brands like Monopoly, Transformers, and Play-Doh.

While competitors and new entrants leverage online platforms and technology integrations, Hasbro has also made strides, including strategic partnerships and digital expansion. However, these moves must translate to tangible revenue growth to justify stock appreciation.
