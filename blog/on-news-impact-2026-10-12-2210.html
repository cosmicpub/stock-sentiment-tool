Growth Catalysts Driving ON Semiconductor

Several growth dynamics make ON Semiconductor a compelling story. First, the shift toward electric vehicles (EVs) fuels demand for specialized power management chips—one of ON’s strengths. ON’s ongoing investments in R&D and manufacturing capacity also bode well for meeting future demand. Its push to enhance product portfolio in sensors and image processing chips strengthens the company’s position in fast-growing segments.
