Understanding NextDecade’s Business Model and Growth Drivers

NextDecade specializes in developing LNG export terminals and natural gas liquefaction projects, primarily aimed at meeting rising international demand for cleaner-burning energy sources. The company’s flagship projects are strategically located along the U.S. Gulf Coast, leveraging proximity to abundant shale gas supplies.

Global energy consumption trends toward greener fuels underpin NextDecade’s growth prospects. As nations pivot away from coal and oil, LNG is poised to gain market share, which bodes well for NextDecade’s expansion plans.
