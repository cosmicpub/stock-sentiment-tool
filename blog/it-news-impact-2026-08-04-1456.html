Understanding Gartner's Business Model and Market Position

Gartner operates in the research and advisory segment, delivering critical insights and advice that businesses use to make technology decisions. Its broad customer base includes enterprises across industries, which provides steady subscription revenues. With technology spending increasingly strategic for enterprises, Gartner’s services remain relevant, albeit amid rising competition from alternative research platforms and consulting firms.
