Industry Overview: Semiconductor Headwinds and Tailwinds

The semiconductor sector is facing a dual landscape. On one hand, persistent global shortages have forced manufacturers to retool and invest heavily in capacity expansions. On the other, rising interest rates worldwide make financing these expansions more expensive and tighten consumer electronics demand.

ON Semiconductor benefits from strong exposure to automotive electronics—a fast-growing segment driven by electric vehicle adoption and advanced driver assistance systems. Nevertheless, broader market uncertainties, including geopolitical conflicts impacting supply chains, pose ongoing challenges.
