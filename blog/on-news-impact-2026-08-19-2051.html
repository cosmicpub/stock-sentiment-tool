Growth Drivers Powering ON Semiconductor

ON Semiconductor's steady innovation and strategic focus on high-growth markets underpin optimism. Key areas include:

  • IoT Expansion: The proliferation of connected devices enhances requirements for low-power, high-performance semiconductor solutions.

These sectors offer relatively resilient growth drivers, which could mitigate traditional semiconductor cyclical swings.
