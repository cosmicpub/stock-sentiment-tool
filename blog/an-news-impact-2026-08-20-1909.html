The retail auto industry is experiencing a pivot toward online sales, electrification, and subscription models. Competition is intensifying, both from traditional automakers adopting direct sales approaches and new entrants redefining the customer experience. AutoNation’s ability to innovate digitally and expand electric vehicle offerings will be pivotal to maintaining its market position.
