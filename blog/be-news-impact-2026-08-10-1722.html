While Bloom’s core fuel cell technology isn’t polysilicon-dependent, supply chain disruptions and geopolitical trade tensions pose uncertainties. Furthermore, the company faces competition from traditional energy providers and emerging technologies, necessitating continued innovation to maintain market share.
