Understanding Two Harbors' Business Model

Two Harbors primarily invests in residential mortgage-backed securities and other related assets. It seeks to generate income from the spread between borrowing costs and yields on mortgage assets. This arbitrage strategy can deliver steady dividends, particularly attractive in a low-yield environment. However, their earnings are sensitive to interest rate fluctuations and mortgage prepayment speeds, requiring active portfolio management.
