This creates predictable cash flows supported by rate-setting authorities but limits rapid expansion potential. The utility’s focus is increasingly shifting toward renewable energy integration, aligning with Hawaii’s ambitious clean energy goals.

This positioning provides stability but also makes the company vulnerable to regulatory risks and capital expenditure demands related to grid modernization and renewable integration.
