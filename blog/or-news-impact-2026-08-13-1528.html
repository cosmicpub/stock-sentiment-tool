Understanding OR Royalties’ Business Model

Unlike traditional miners, OR Royalties operates primarily by acquiring royalty interests on mining projects, enabling it to earn revenue without bearing the direct operational and exploration risks. This model can protect cash flow during downturns but also limits upside during commodity booms.

Investors should appreciate how this structure can smooth earnings volatility but still remain sensitive to metal prices, such as copper, gold, and silver. Given recent market flux, OR's revenue streams may face pressure if mining operations slow or metal prices decline significantly.
