Why Investors Are Bullish on ARE

Potential Risks to Watch

While ARE's niche focus is a strength, it also exposes the company to sector-specific risks. Biotech can be cyclical, with potential knock-on effects on lease renewals if companies face funding crunches or clinical setbacks.

Interest rate sensitivity is another consideration. As a real estate investment trust, ARE’s cost of capital rises with rate hikes, which could pressure profitability or valuations if borrowing costs escalate sharply.

Moreover, competition in the life sciences real estate arena is increasing, with new entrants expanding their footprints, which could dampen future rental growth.
