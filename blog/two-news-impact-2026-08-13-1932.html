Understanding Two Harbors’ Business Model

Two Harbors invests predominantly in mortgage-backed securities, profiting from the spread between borrowing costs and yield on its assets. This “spread income” can be attractive but is susceptible to fluctuations in interest rates and prepayment speeds. Additionally, TWO actively manages credit and interest rate risks, which adds complexity to its income profile.
