What Makes Safehold’s Business Model Distinctive?

Safehold disrupts typical real estate investing by owning ground leases rather than physical buildings. This model ensures recurring revenue through long-term lease agreements without bearing maintenance or redevelopment risks. In volatile economic climates, such steady cash flows can act as an anchor amid fluctuating real estate valuations.
