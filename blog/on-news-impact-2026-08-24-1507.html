Trade Tariffs: A Heavy Weight on ON Semiconductor

One of the biggest catalysts behind the recent negative sentiment is the Trump administration's announced plan to increase U.S. tariffs on imported cars, trucks, and auto parts from Canada to a striking 50% starting January 1, 2027. Given ON Semiconductor's exposure to the automotive semiconductor market, this policy shift poses a direct threat. The potential for increased costs and disrupted supply chains could suppress profitability and disrupt order flows from automotive clients.

ON Semiconductor has carved a niche supplying power and automotive semiconductors, which is a growth segment but highly sensitive to regulatory and tariff pressures. Compared to peers focusing on memory chips or general-purpose semiconductors, ON’s specialty makes it more vulnerable to specific trade disputes affecting auto parts.
